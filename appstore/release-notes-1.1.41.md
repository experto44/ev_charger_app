Written for drivers, not developers: what changed for them, no mechanics.

## App Store — English only

Charger status now updates much faster.

• Tap refresh and you get an answer in about a second. The app asks the charging operator directly, so you see what is happening right now instead of a reading that could be several minutes old.

• The refresh button now tells you what actually happened: whether something changed, whether there was nothing new to report, or whether the check could not go through. Before, it said "Updated" every time.

• An open charger card keeps itself up to date, so you no longer need to close it and open it again to see the latest status.

• "Free again" alerts arrive sooner after a plug is unplugged.

• Georgia, Turkey and Armenia are now at the top of the country list in Settings.

• The provider list is tidier. Turkey and International only appear once you have picked those countries in Settings, and they switch on by themselves when you do.

• The app uses less mobile data while it is open.
