# Release notes — 1.1.47 (build 59)

Written for drivers, not developers: what changed for them, no mechanics.
Google Play caps "What's new" at 500 characters per language; the App Store
allows 4000. Both versions below are within their limit.

Covers 1.1.42 through 1.1.47, since 1.1.41 was the last release with notes.

Deliberately not mentioned: the subscription entitlement fixes. Nothing there
changes anything a paying user can see, and the bug they close is not something
to advertise.

## Google Play — English

New: get a heads-up when a provider opens a new charging station. Turn it on in Settings.

• The map background is back.
• Charger status is shown only when the operator actually reports it. No more invented readings.
• When a plug shows no status, tap to find out why and what it means for you.
• Subscription screen fixes: prices load properly and error messages are readable again.

---

## App Store — English only

New: find out about new chargers the day they open.

• Turn on "New Chargers" in Settings and the app tells you when a provider opens a new station. Tap the alert and it takes you straight to that charger on the map.

• The map background is back. A change at our map provider had left it blank for some drivers.

• Charger status is now honest. For some Tegeta chargers the app was showing a live "free" or "busy" reading that the operator never actually sent. Those plugs now say the status is not published, rather than telling you something that might be wrong.

• When a plug has no live status, you can tap to find out why. The app explains that the operator does not share live data for that charger, that we still know the charger is there, and why no price is shown for it either.

• Two chargers that are no longer in service have been removed from the map.

• Fixes on the subscription screen. Prices now load reliably, a plan that cannot be purchased in your country is no longer offered, and if something does go wrong the message explaining it is now readable instead of appearing as an empty bar.
