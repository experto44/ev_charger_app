# Release notes — 1.1.50 (build 62)

Written for drivers, not developers: what changed for them, no mechanics.
Google Play caps "What's new" at 500 characters per language; the App Store
allows 4000. Both versions below are within their limit.

This release is one new thing plus two fixes that came along with it:

* **Expenses** — a log of what charging actually costs, in the profile. A paid
  charge is the amount the driver paid in the provider's own app; a home charge
  is worked out from the percentage they started and finished at, using their
  own battery size and electricity tariff.
* Routes shared out of the older Google Maps link format are accepted again.
* Route planning no longer asks Google for the same trip three times, which is
  a billing fix and says nothing to the driver. Not mentioned in store text.

The expenses log lives under Profile, which requires signing in, so the store
text should not promise it to signed-out users.

## Google Play — English

New: Expenses. Keep track of what charging really costs you.

Tap the plus after a charge. Paid at a charger? Type what you paid. Charged at
home? Type the percentage you started and finished at and the app works the
cost out from your own battery and electricity tariff.

Monthly totals, all-time totals, and a home against paid breakdown, so you can
see where the money goes.

## App Store — English

Expenses: see what charging is really costing you.

• Charged at a paid charger? Tap the plus, choose "At a paid charger" and type
  the amount you paid in that provider's app. That is the whole entry.

• Charged at home? Choose "At home" and type the battery percentage you started
  and finished at. GeoCharge works the cost out from your battery size, your
  electricity tariff and the energy lost while charging, which you fill in once.

• The screen adds up this month and all time, splits home charging from paid
  charging, and shows the last six months side by side.

• Every record keeps the date it happened on, and can be edited or deleted.

• Records are stored on your phone and follow your account, so a new phone
  starts with your history already there.

Expenses lives under Profile. Also in this release: routes shared out of Google
Maps are read correctly again when the link comes in the older format.
