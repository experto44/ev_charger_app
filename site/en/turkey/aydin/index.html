Frequently asked questions

How many chargers are there in Aydın?

234 public stations: 145 fast DC and 89 standard AC, run by 46 operators. The largest is ZES with 49 stations.

Which connectors are used in Aydın?

CCS2 (145), Type 2 (110), CHAdeMO (9). You need CCS2 for fast charging.

What does fast charging cost in Aydın?

The median DC tariff is 14.74 ₺ per kWh. Operators publish one national tariff, so the price is effectively the same as in the rest of the country.
