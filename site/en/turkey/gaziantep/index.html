Frequently asked questions

How many chargers are there in Gaziantep?

136 public stations: 93 fast DC and 43 standard AC, run by 27 operators. The largest is En Yakıt with 29 stations.

Which connectors are used in Gaziantep?

CCS2 (93), Type 2 (56), CHAdeMO (6). You need CCS2 for fast charging.

What does fast charging cost in Gaziantep?

The median DC tariff is 14.90 ₺ per kWh. Operators publish one national tariff, so the price is effectively the same as in the rest of the country.
