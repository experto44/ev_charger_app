Frequently asked questions

How many chargers are there in Erzurum?

72 public stations: 53 fast DC and 19 standard AC, run by 21 operators. The largest is Trugo with 32 stations.

Which connectors are used in Erzurum?

CCS2 (53), Type 2 (25). You need CCS2 for fast charging.

What does fast charging cost in Erzurum?

The median DC tariff is 14.98 ₺ per kWh. Operators publish one national tariff, so the price is effectively the same as in the rest of the country.
