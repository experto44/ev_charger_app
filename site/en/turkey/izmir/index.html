Frequently asked questions

How many chargers are there in İzmir?

604 public stations: 347 fast DC and 257 standard AC, run by 69 operators. The largest is ZES with 111 stations.

Which connectors are used in İzmir?

CCS2 (347), Type 2 (335), CHAdeMO (11). You need CCS2 for fast charging.

What does fast charging cost in İzmir?

The median DC tariff is 14.74 ₺ per kWh. Operators publish one national tariff, so the price is effectively the same as in the rest of the country.
