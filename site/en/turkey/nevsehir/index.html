Frequently asked questions

How many chargers are there in Nevşehir?

131 public stations: 56 fast DC and 75 standard AC, run by 34 operators. The largest is ZES with 20 stations.

Which connectors are used in Nevşehir?

Type 2 (86), CCS2 (56), CHAdeMO (2). You need CCS2 for fast charging.

What does fast charging cost in Nevşehir?

The median DC tariff is 13.99 ₺ per kWh. Operators publish one national tariff, so the price is effectively the same as in the rest of the country.
