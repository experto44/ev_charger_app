Frequently asked questions

How many chargers are there in Ordu?

85 public stations: 54 fast DC and 31 standard AC, run by 21 operators. The largest is Trugo with 12 stations.

Which connectors are used in Ordu?

CCS2 (54), Type 2 (43), CHAdeMO (1). You need CCS2 for fast charging.

What does fast charging cost in Ordu?

The median DC tariff is 13.90 ₺ per kWh. Operators publish one national tariff, so the price is effectively the same as in the rest of the country.
