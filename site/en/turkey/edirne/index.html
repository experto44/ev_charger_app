Frequently asked questions

How many chargers are there in Edirne?

89 public stations: 56 fast DC and 33 standard AC, run by 27 operators. The largest is ZES with 22 stations.

Which connectors are used in Edirne?

CCS2 (56), Type 2 (39), CHAdeMO (3). You need CCS2 for fast charging.

What does fast charging cost in Edirne?

The median DC tariff is 14.74 ₺ per kWh. Operators publish one national tariff, so the price is effectively the same as in the rest of the country.
