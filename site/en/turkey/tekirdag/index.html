Frequently asked questions

How many chargers are there in Tekirdağ?

176 public stations: 112 fast DC and 64 standard AC, run by 45 operators. The largest is ZES with 30 stations.

Which connectors are used in Tekirdağ?

CCS2 (112), Type 2 (79), CHAdeMO (2). You need CCS2 for fast charging.

What does fast charging cost in Tekirdağ?

The median DC tariff is 13.99 ₺ per kWh. Operators publish one national tariff, so the price is effectively the same as in the rest of the country.
