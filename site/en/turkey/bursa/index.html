Frequently asked questions

How many chargers are there in Bursa?

610 public stations: 334 fast DC and 276 standard AC, run by 68 operators. The largest is ZES with 98 stations.

Which connectors are used in Bursa?

Type 2 (348), CCS2 (333), CHAdeMO (10). You need CCS2 for fast charging.

What does fast charging cost in Bursa?

The median DC tariff is 14.74 ₺ per kWh. Operators publish one national tariff, so the price is effectively the same as in the rest of the country.
