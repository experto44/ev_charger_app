Frequently asked questions

How many chargers are there in Adana?

203 public stations: 139 fast DC and 64 standard AC, run by 26 operators. The largest is Voltgo with 43 stations.

Which connectors are used in Adana?

CCS2 (139), Type 2 (94). You need CCS2 for fast charging.

What does fast charging cost in Adana?

The median DC tariff is 13.90 ₺ per kWh. Operators publish one national tariff, so the price is effectively the same as in the rest of the country.
