Frequently asked questions

How many chargers are there in Diyarbakır?

141 public stations: 81 fast DC and 60 standard AC, run by 29 operators. The largest is OtoPriz with 22 stations.

Which connectors are used in Diyarbakır?

CCS2 (81), Type 2 (74), CHAdeMO (2). You need CCS2 for fast charging.

What does fast charging cost in Diyarbakır?

The median DC tariff is 14.90 ₺ per kWh. Operators publish one national tariff, so the price is effectively the same as in the rest of the country.
