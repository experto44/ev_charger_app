Frequently asked questions

How many chargers are there in Kayseri?

352 public stations: 245 fast DC and 107 standard AC, run by 42 operators. The largest is K Şarj with 133 stations.

Which connectors are used in Kayseri?

Type 2 (261), CCS2 (245). You need CCS2 for fast charging.

What does fast charging cost in Kayseri?

The median DC tariff is 11.65 ₺ per kWh. Operators publish one national tariff, so the price is effectively the same as in the rest of the country.
