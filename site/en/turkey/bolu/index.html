Frequently asked questions

How many chargers are there in Bolu?

103 public stations: 69 fast DC and 34 standard AC, run by 37 operators. The largest is ZES with 18 stations.

Which connectors are used in Bolu?

CCS2 (69), Type 2 (42), CHAdeMO (2). You need CCS2 for fast charging.

What does fast charging cost in Bolu?

The median DC tariff is 13.99 ₺ per kWh. Operators publish one national tariff, so the price is effectively the same as in the rest of the country.
