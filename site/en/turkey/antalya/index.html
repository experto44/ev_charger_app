Frequently asked questions

How many chargers are there in Antalya?

834 public stations: 398 fast DC and 436 standard AC, run by 70 operators. The largest is ZES with 186 stations.

Which connectors are used in Antalya?

Type 2 (537), CCS2 (398), CHAdeMO (7). You need CCS2 for fast charging.

What does fast charging cost in Antalya?

The median DC tariff is 14.74 ₺ per kWh. Operators publish one national tariff, so the price is effectively the same as in the rest of the country.
