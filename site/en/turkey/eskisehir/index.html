Frequently asked questions

How many chargers are there in Eskişehir?

157 public stations: 94 fast DC and 63 standard AC, run by 29 operators. The largest is ZES with 66 stations.

Which connectors are used in Eskişehir?

CCS2 (94), Type 2 (83), CHAdeMO (3). You need CCS2 for fast charging.

What does fast charging cost in Eskişehir?

The median DC tariff is 14.74 ₺ per kWh. Operators publish one national tariff, so the price is effectively the same as in the rest of the country.
