Frequently asked questions

How many chargers are there in Denizli?

228 public stations: 147 fast DC and 81 standard AC, run by 47 operators. The largest is ZES with 31 stations.

Which connectors are used in Denizli?

CCS2 (147), Type 2 (107), CHAdeMO (6). You need CCS2 for fast charging.

What does fast charging cost in Denizli?

The median DC tariff is 14.74 ₺ per kWh. Operators publish one national tariff, so the price is effectively the same as in the rest of the country.
