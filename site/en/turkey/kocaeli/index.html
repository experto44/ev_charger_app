Frequently asked questions

How many chargers are there in Kocaeli?

311 public stations: 190 fast DC and 121 standard AC, run by 63 operators. The largest is Trugo with 36 stations.

Which connectors are used in Kocaeli?

CCS2 (190), Type 2 (157), CHAdeMO (3). You need CCS2 for fast charging.

What does fast charging cost in Kocaeli?

The median DC tariff is 14.74 ₺ per kWh. Operators publish one national tariff, so the price is effectively the same as in the rest of the country.
