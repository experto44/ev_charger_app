Frequently asked questions

How many chargers are there in Sakarya?

226 public stations: 129 fast DC and 97 standard AC, run by 50 operators. The largest is Trugo with 27 stations.

Which connectors are used in Sakarya?

CCS2 (129), Type 2 (122), CHAdeMO (3). You need CCS2 for fast charging.

What does fast charging cost in Sakarya?

The median DC tariff is 13.99 ₺ per kWh. Operators publish one national tariff, so the price is effectively the same as in the rest of the country.
