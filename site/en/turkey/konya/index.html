Frequently asked questions

How many chargers are there in Konya?

339 public stations: 189 fast DC and 150 standard AC, run by 52 operators. The largest is ZES with 53 stations.

Which connectors are used in Konya?

Type 2 (192), CCS2 (189), CHAdeMO (7). You need CCS2 for fast charging.

What does fast charging cost in Konya?

The median DC tariff is 14.74 ₺ per kWh. Operators publish one national tariff, so the price is effectively the same as in the rest of the country.
