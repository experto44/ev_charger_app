Frequently asked questions

How many chargers are there in Mersin?

220 public stations: 147 fast DC and 73 standard AC, run by 36 operators. The largest is Eşarj with 26 stations.

Which connectors are used in Mersin?

CCS2 (147), Type 2 (96), CHAdeMO (7). You need CCS2 for fast charging.

What does fast charging cost in Mersin?

The median DC tariff is 13.99 ₺ per kWh. Operators publish one national tariff, so the price is effectively the same as in the rest of the country.
