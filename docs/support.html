Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I find chargers near me?
    Open the app and allow location access — the map centers on your position and shows nearby charging stations. You can also search any address or destination.
  • What does “Plan & Go” do?
    Enter your destination and current battery level. GeoCharge suggests charging stops along your route, shows the battery you'll have on arrival, and opens your chosen stops in Google Maps.
  • Is availability real-time?
    Availability is shown where the charging provider publishes it. It reflects the provider's most recent update and may not be live to the second.
  • What is GeoCharge Premium?
    Premium removes all ads. It's a monthly or yearly auto-renewable subscription with a 7-day free trial, manageable in your App Store settings.
  • How do I delete my account?
    In the app, go to Profile → Delete Account. This removes your account and all associated data immediately.
