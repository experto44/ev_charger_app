Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

Overview

GeoCharge ("we", "our", or "us") is an EV charging station map application for Georgia and the surrounding region. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how you can control it. We are committed to being transparent and keeping your data safe.

Data We Collect

DataDetails
Email address Collected when you create an account or sign in with Google. Used to identify your account and send service notifications.
Phone number Optionally provided by you in the profile screen. Stored as a Georgian mobile number (+995 prefix). Never shared with third parties.
Location Accessed while the app is in use to center the map on your position and calculate distances to chargers. Not stored on our servers.
Device information Basic device data (OS version, device model) collected automatically by Firebase for crash reporting and analytics.
Usage data Anonymous interaction data (screens visited, features used) to help us improve the app. No personally identifiable information is included.

Why We Collect It

  • Account management — to create and secure your GeoCharge account, and allow you to sign in across devices.
  • App functionality — location is used solely to show nearby chargers and calculate routes. It is never stored or shared.
  • Service notifications — your email and (optionally) phone number may be used to inform you of important service updates, such as changes to charger availability in your area.
  • App improvement — anonymous crash reports and usage statistics help us identify and fix bugs and prioritise new features.

Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Google) — we use Firebase Authentication for sign-in, Cloud Firestore to store your profile data, and Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics for app diagnostics. Data is processed by Google under their Privacy Policy.
  • Google Maps / Google Places API — used for the address search feature and navigation links. Queries are sent to Google's servers. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Open Charge Map — international charging station data is fetched from the Open Charge Map public API. No personal data is transmitted.
  • CartoDB — map tile images are served by CartoDB (CARTO). No personal data is transmitted in tile requests.

Data Retention

We retain your account data (email, phone number) for as long as your account is active. Anonymous usage and crash data is retained for up to 12 months. Location data is never stored.

Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To request data deletion or a copy of your data:

  • Delete your account directly within the app (Profile → Sign Out, then contact us to remove Firestore data).
  • Email us at geocharge.app@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". We will process your request within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

GeoCharge is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
